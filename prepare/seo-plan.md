# Heya: The AGI Orchestration Layer for the Future of Work

## 1. Executive Summary
The goal of this SEO plan is to position **Heya** not just as a tool, but as the definitive **AGI Orchestration Layer**. We will leverage the massive search volume of leading models (Gemini, GPT-4, Claude, DeepSeek) and the rising trend of "AI Employees" to dominate the conversation around the **Autonomous AI Workforce**.

## 2. Trend-Jacking & Visionary Keyword Strategy
### High-Traffic "Trend-Jacking" Keywords
- **Gemini 1.5 Pro vs GPT-4o**: "How to orchestrate Gemini and GPT-4 in a single team."
- **DeepSeek V3 / Qwen 2.5**: "The best platform for DeepSeek-powered multi-agent collaboration."
- **Claude 3.5 Sonnet**: "Unlocking Claude's reasoning in a multi-agent environment."
- **AutoGPT / BabyAGI Evolution**: "The next step beyond simple autonomous agents."

### "Grand Vision" Keywords
- **AGI Orchestration**: Positioning Heya as the brain that manages AGI-level models.
- **AI Employee Workforce**: "Build your first 1,000 AI employees with Heya."
- **Collective Intelligence / Swarm AI**: Moving beyond individual chat to hive-mind problem solving.
- **The Post-Chat Era**: Defining the transition from "Chatting with AI" to "Collaborating with AI Teams."
- **Human-AI Symbiosis**: The ultimate workspace for the hybrid future.

## 3. On-Page SEO: "The Authority" Positioning
### Meta Tags & Titles
- **Home Page**: `Heya | The AGI Orchestration Layer & Autonomous AI Workforce`
- **Description**: `Stop chatting, start orchestrating. Heya is the world's first multi-agent workspace where Gemini, GPT-4, and DeepSeek collaborate as your specialized AI Employee team. The future of AGI starts here.`

### Semantic HTML & Content
- **H1**: `The Evolution of Intelligence: From Chatbots to AI Workforces.`
- **H2**: `Why One AI Isn't Enough: The Case for Multi-Agent Swarm Intelligence.`
